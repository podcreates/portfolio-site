FAQ

Quick answers — so you know exactly how the process works.

What types of thumbnails do you design?
I design high-impact thumbnails focused on clarity, emotion, and clickability — including IRL, gaming, documentary, sports, and creator-focused content. Every design is built to communicate the idea instantly.
How long does it take to deliver a thumbnail?
Most thumbnails are delivered within 24 hours. Faster delivery is often possible depending on workload.
Do you offer revisions?
Yes. Every project includes revisions to make sure the thumbnail is exactly what you’re looking for.
What niches do you specialize in?
IRL content, gaming, documentaries, football/sports, and creator-focused content — but I’m open to other niches too.
How does pricing work?
Pricing is most often monthly-based, billed at the end of the month for all thumbnails created. Per-project pricing is also available.
